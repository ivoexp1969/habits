Habits helps you build and keep good routines — simple, beautiful and clutter-free.

  Track your daily goals, keep streaks alive and watch your progress with clear statistics. Works fully offline.

  🎯 KEY FEATURES

  • Daily habit tracking — mark each habit done with a single tap
  • 🔥 Streaks — see how many days in a row you've kept a habit
  • 📊 Stats & charts — weekly progress, levels and XP to keep you motivated
  • 📅 Calendar — see the whole month with a summary of completed days and your best streak
  • 🏆 Achievements — unlock rewards as you progress
  • ⏰ Smart reminders — get notified at the right moment if you're falling behind
  • 🎉 Perfect-day confetti — a little reward when you complete everything
  • 🎵 Relaxing music with a sleep timer
  • 💾 Backup & restore — export your data and restore it anytime
  • 🌗 Light and dark themes

  📦 READY-MADE HABIT PACKS

  Get started fast with curated bundles — health, productivity, fitness, balance and more. Add a whole pack with one
  tap, with no duplicate habits.

  🔒 PRIVACY

  Your data stays locally on your device. The app works without an internet connection.

  💙 FREE

  All features are completely free. The app is supported by non-intrusive ads — if you prefer, you can remove them with
  a one-time purchase in settings.
